And once there is a reference, there is room for opinion, because a measurement can be objective or subjective. When I say "today is a hot day", I can mean two very different things. I can mean that today is warmer than the usual temperature recorded for this time of year. That's objective. Or I can simply mean: I feel hot. That's subjective. Notice that almost nobody carries the weather records around in their head. So how do we decide, so confidently, that a day is hot?

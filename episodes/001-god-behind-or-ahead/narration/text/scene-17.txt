I'm an engineer, so I can't help thinking about this like a project. We may already be holding the blueprint. We know what the finished building is supposed to look like, because we described it in our religions thousands of years ago. What we didn't see is that construction was already under way: every species that learned something, every generation that passed it on, laid one more brick. I don't know if we'll be around for the opening. But I find it strangely comforting to think we're on the crew.

Here is a question that sounds too simple to be interesting. Can you measure anything on its own without having a reference? A distance, a temperature, intelligence? Hold on to that question, because if we follow it honestly step by step, it takes us somewhere I did not expect. Say you are standing ten metres away from me. I am the starting point, the zero on the tape. Now walk over to me, hand me the tape, and measure the same gap from where you stand. Same distance, different reference. Nothing is measured absolutely. Every number we ever write down quietly carries a second piece of information: the place it was measured from.

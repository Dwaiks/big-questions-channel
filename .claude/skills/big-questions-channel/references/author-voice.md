# The author's voice

Source: the author's blog (https://oliverkaspard.blogspot.com, 11 posts, 2019–2022). Read the original post whenever an episode comes from it.

## How the author thinks
- Explains life with physics and measurement: time as counting repeating motions, laziness as inertia, "hot" as a comparison, waste as lost time.
- Keeps noticing that things are relative ("absolutely relative, btw", "totally relative"): this is the root of the channel lens.
- Engineer's habits: efficiency, minimum friction, rates and units (km/h, euros per hour), cause and effect.
- Moves from a personal story to a general idea, often ending on an open question.

## Stories the author has told (use only as written)
- 2001, All Saints' Day: hunting trip in Laqlouq, Mount Lebanon (1,780 m), host Jihad Younes, the moon "so close you could touch it", a heavy wool quilt, the lone pear on a bare tree near the top of the hill. (High hanging fruits)
- 2016: working briefly in a restaurant kitchen; the anger at wasted food was really about wasted time. Grows bonsai trees and mushrooms, draws, cooks, writes. (Waste)
- The author's sister's answer to the Ship of Theseus: identity is the voyages, not the planks. (The Ship of Theseus)
- A boss who was never impressed and the snacks that replaced recognition. (The dog training effect)
- Biking 10 km to the gym; a trainer friend's line that the good feeling comes after. (Overcome your mental inertia)
- Lives in Munich (Nymphenburg, Starnberger See and Feldmochinger See appear in posts); Lebanese roots.

Use personal details sparingly and only when they serve the episode. Ask the author before using anything about other named people.

## Writing style to keep
- Direct, conversational, sometimes humorous asides.
- Concrete objects and senses (the smell of cold air, dew on the face, a jar kept since 2016).
- Short punchy conclusions.

## Writing style to adjust for video
- The blog sometimes rambles and repeats; scripts keep one line of reasoning.
- Fix spelling and grammar; keep the author's phrasing where it is vivid.
- Remove health, diet, smoking and mental-health advice (AI narrator rule) and anything about named private people.

## Off-limits posts for the channel
- "L'appel du vide" (suicide framing). Its idea "curiosity, not hope, keeps us going" may be used alone, without that framing.
- "My GF and I agreed on a cheat day!" (personal, diet).
- "So why bother anyway?" and "Climbing the mountain" are material for "My view" paragraphs only.

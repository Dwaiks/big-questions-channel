# YouTube rules the channel stays inside

Checked 22 Nov 2026 through secondary sources; confirm on YouTube's own help pages (https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851) when anything is decided on them. Re-check every few months: these rules change.

## Earning
- Partner Program today: 1,000 subscribers + 4,000 public watch hours (12 months) or 10M Shorts views (90 days).
- From 1 Feb 2027, new applicants: 1,000 subscribers + 8,000 watch hours or 20M Shorts views. Shorts watch time doesn't count toward hours. Members who joined earlier keep their status.
- Lower tier (memberships, Super Thanks, Shopping): 500 subscribers, 3 uploads in 90 days, 3,000 hours or 3M Shorts views.

## "Inauthentic content" (since July 2025, clarified July 2026)
Mass-produced, repetitive or template-based videos can't earn, whatever tool made them. AI personas talking about health or finance are singled out.
What that means for us:
- Every video has its own argument, the author's "My view" and illustrations made for that story.
- No reused scene packs, stock filler or the same visual sequence across videos. A 3–5 second channel intro is fine.
- Titles and thumbnails vary in composition, not only in words.

## AI narrator
- Avoid topics that give health, diet, medical, mental-health or personal-finance advice.
- Never present the narrator as a real person or as the author speaking unless it is the author's own cloned voice and they agreed.

## Disclosure
- Realistic synthetic scenes (looks like real footage of real places, people or events) need the "altered or synthetic content" box in YouTube Studio.
- Clearly cartoon illustrations normally don't. When unsure, tick it.

## Accuracy
- Sources go in every description.
- Disputed or popular-but-wrong claims are flagged in the brief and either corrected or framed as "often said".
- Forecasts are stated as forecasts ("some predict…").

## Music and assets
- One licensed music track per video from the subscription library; keep the licence record.
- Illustrations are generated new for each story; no copyrighted characters.
